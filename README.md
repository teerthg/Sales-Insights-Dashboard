Sales Insights Dashboard

This project involves creating an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** using **Power BI**. The dashboard visualizes sales data across different segments, categories, and months, providing insights into sales trends, customer performance, product performance, and more. The dashboard is designed to give users an in-depth understanding of the sales performance and help in strategic decision-making.

## Features:
- **Monthly Order Count**: Displays the number of orders placed each month throughout the year.
- **Sales by Segment and Category**: A bar chart showing sales by different product segments (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).
- **Monthly Sales Trend**: A line chart that visualizes the sales trend across the months.
- **Top 10 Customers by Sales**: A bar chart showing the top customers based on the total sales value.
- **Top 10 Customers by Profit**: A bar chart showing the top customers by profit contribution.
- **Top 10 Products by Profit**: A bar chart showing the top products that generated the most profit.

## Dataset:
The dataset used for this dashboard contains sales data for various products, customers, and segments. Key columns include:
- **Order Number**: The unique identifier for each order.
- **Product Category**: The category of the product (e.g., Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).
- **Customer Name**: The name of the customer who placed the order.
- **Sales**: The total sales amount for the order.
- **Profit**: The profit generated from the order.
- **Order Date**: The date when the order was placed.
- **Order Priority**: The priority level of the order (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

## Power BI Visualizations:
- **Bar Charts**: Used for visualizing monthly orders, sales by segment and category, top customers by sales, and products by profit.
- **Line Chart**: Used to analyze monthly sales trends over the year.
- **Filters**: Filters for selecting data based on year, quarter, product sub-category, and order priority.
